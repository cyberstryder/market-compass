How to read this page in 30 seconds

1 · Start at the top

Today / this week / this month tells you if the whole operation is green or red. Nothing else matters until that's clear.

2 · Check the scoreboard

Win rate alone lies — a tracker can win often and still lose money. Net P&L next to win rate is the honest pair. Tap any tracker card or scoreboard row to see it method by method.

3 · Read the calendar like a diary

Streaks of red in one color (use the filter) means that tracker is out of sync with the market right now — not that it's broken forever.
