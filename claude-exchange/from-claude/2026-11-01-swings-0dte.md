# Swings and 0DTE — why they can't "work" as built, and what the signals are actually worth (Claude, 2026-11-01)

Basis: main `09ba9da`; `data-foundation-v2-stocks` (163 symbols, 1-min SIP, 2024-10-22 → 2026-10-29, 43.9M bars).
Both books were replayed **on the underlying** with the production rules copied line-for-line from `swing_signals.py`
and `scanner.py`. Harness + trade CSVs in `2026-11-01-swings-0dte/`. Nothing here changes a live or paper seat.

## 1. Why neither book has ever produced a measured result

Both books are live forward-tests that buy an option and then score the option's quote path. Every gate sits on the
option quote, not the signal:

| | Swings (`swing_ideas.py`) | 0DTE (`zero_dte_paper.py` + `scanner_shadow.py`) |
|---|---|---|
| Admission | fresh underlying quote **and** complete chain **and** streamed option quote ≤5 s old, two-sided, spread ≤10% / $0.30, bid ≥ $0.10, within 0.25 ATR of signal | alert → fresh option quote ≤120 s old, else `data_gap` row |
| Stream capacity | 400–500 contracts shared across every book, for a 144–163-name universe; single-name contracts print every 30–120 s | same stream |
| Record to date | 127 tickets, **0 conversions**, 123 died waiting for a quote (Quill 10-27); session-end fuse shipped 10-27 | 11,311 tickets / week, **30 observed**, 81% window-ended; seated cells (`session_sweep_reclaim` × AMD/MU/NFLX) booked zero all week |
| Exit model | 0.65×/1.75× premium, 10-session hold, daily-close snapshot (which itself needs a chain + quote at the close) | ±50% premium or 14:55 CT flatten; quoteless exits → `data_gap`, excluded |

So "can't get it working" is the architecture: the sample size is set by option-quote arrivals, not by signals, and the
exclusion machinery removes the observations that do arrive. No change to fuses, windows or seats fixes that.
The only thing that can be measured today is the signal on the underlying — which is what the data releases exist
for and what neither book had ever done. Done below.

## 2. Swings — three rules × two sides, 2 years × 163 names

Method: `daily_context` and `technical_setups` reproduced exactly (60 contiguous sessions + 10 complete weeks,
SMA20/50, weekly SMA10, Wilder RSI14/ATR14, 20- and 5-session extremes; 1-min close crosses the daily level vs the
prior minute; signal before 14:30 CT). Entry next-bar open ±$0.01; stop = production invalidation, target 2R, bar
extremes, stop-first; max hold 10 sessions, out 15 min before that close; also fixed horizons (close of
entry day, +1/+3/+5/+9 session closes = the swing-study checkpoints). Primary sample = first fire per
symbol-day-rule-side (13,253; all fires 62,776 — median 4 re-fires per symbol-day, so production's "every trigger bar
is a separate experiment" overcounts ~5×). Placebo = same symbol, random other session, same clock minute, same side,
same stop as a multiple of that day's ATR14, same 2R/10-session geometry, 20 draws/trade, 2,000 resampled cell means.
t is day-clustered.

Readings:
- **Five of six cells are worse than a random entry at the same time with the same geometry.** Breakouts and reclaims
  are the Compass pattern again: buy the cross, get the 20-session high as the entry and the mean reversion as the exit.
  Every short cell loses to its own placebo in a two-year up-market, and the shorts are worse than "the market went up"
  explains (excess −0.03 to −0.16 R).
- **`daily_reversal` long is the one cell with excess**: +0.14 R/trade over placebo, stop rate 39%, WR 49% vs 44%
  placebo, robust to dropping the top 2% of trades (0.125) and either big quarter (2025Q2 0.126, 2026Q2 0.17), present in
  both halves. The excess appears only at 5–9 sessions (s1/s3 are at placebo: +5/+22 vs +9/+33 bps; s5 +88 vs +48;
  s9 +201 vs +79). That is 1–2-week mean reversion in names below SMA20 < SMA50 with RSI 25–55 — the short-term
  reversal effect, not anything about the minute cross.
- **It decays.** Last 6 months (May–Oct 2026, n = 858): R +0.064 vs placebo +0.051; s5 +28 vs +54; s9 +69 vs +91.
  The whole excess is pre-May 2026 (2025Q2 and 2026Q1–Q2 dip recoveries). 6 cells tested; the R percentile survives
  a 6-way correction, the s5 percentile (97) does not.
- **The option would eat it.** Mean stop is 1.5 ATR ≈ 6.7% of price; the excess is ~90 bps on the stock over ~7
  sessions. On a 30–45 DTE near-ATM call (delta ~0.5, premium ~5–6% of spot at 40% IV) that is ≈ +8% of premium in
  directional gain against ≈ 8% theta over the same seven sessions, before spread and before the 0.65× premium stop
  fires on normal noise (median R is −0.03). The production expression is roughly theta-neutral on a signal that is
  itself fading.

What to do with the swing book:
1. Turn off `daily_breakout` and `pullback_reclaim` (both sides) and `daily_reversal` short. They are placebo-negative
   on 2 years × 163 names; nothing a longer forward window can change.
2. `daily_reversal` long: register it as the one swing hypothesis, **on shares, personal account**, with the
   production geometry, and watch the last-6-month decay. If it is run as an option at all: 60–90 DTE, delta ≥ 0.7, no
   premium stop (the underlying stop is the stop), so theta and premium noise stop deciding the outcome.
3. Stop scoring option quote paths. The underlying R and the 5/9-session returns are the metric; the option is an
   execution question to be priced from the `chain_eod` snapshots once there are enough of them (there are ~5 days).
4. Deduplicate fires: one experiment per symbol-day-rule-side. The ledger's "separate trigger bars are separate
   experiments" inflates n ~5× with the same trade.

## 3. 0DTE — the seated family on the underlying

`session_sweep_reclaim` (the only seated/promoted scanner family) reproduced from `scanner.py` + `market.levels`:
prior-RTH high/low (complete prior session) and overnight high/low (extended-hours bars in [open−15.5 h, open));
long = bar low < level − tick, close > level, close > prior-bar high; stop = bar low − tick; mirrored short; signal
before 14:30 CT. Scored at +30/+60/+120 min and the 14:55 flatten bar, and with stop = invalidation / 2R target.
First fire per symbol-day-side: 86,972 (all fires 382,069 — **754 fires per day across the universe**; that is the
11k-ticket week).

Readings:
- The signal has **no information on the underlying**: every cell is within a few bps of zero at every horizon,
  pooled slightly negative (−3 to −4 bps gross per trade, t −2 to −8 on 87k trades — i.e. a real, tiny anti-edge),
  win rate at the 1:2 bracket is the 33% a coin gives. The 2025 → 2026 halves agree. The seated three names are the
  same as the universe.
- The mean stop is 40 bps of price. A 0DTE option's spread alone is a larger fraction of premium than a 40 bps spot
  move is of delta. The ±50% premium exit on a signal with 0 bps of drift is a negative-expectation bet by spread and
  theta, as the 10-26 review said; this replay removes the remaining question of whether the signal was hiding behind
  the quote machinery. It was not.
- Fire rate: 754/day on 163 names. A quote stream of 400–500 contracts cannot observe that; nor should it have to.

What to do with 0DTE: unseat `session_sweep_reclaim`; stop opening shadow records on it; do not spend quote capacity
on it. The other five scanner families (`orb_retest`, `trend_pullback`, `volume_breakout`, `level_breakdown`,
`exposure_level_break`) can be replayed on the same data with the same harness in under an hour each; the futures
ORB/VWAP results and this one say what to expect. If a 0DTE book is to exist, it has to start from a signal that
shows drift on SPY/QQQ at a fixed horizon on the underlying (the only 0DTE idea with literature is the
first-30 → last-30 intraday momentum, which the MES sweep put at ≈ $5–12 gross/day), and price the option off
`chain_eod` + Black-Scholes between prints — not from a 1 Hz single-name stream.

## 4. Caveats

In-sample, one two-year period (strong up-market with V-shaped dips: the swing long cells' placebo baselines are
+50 bps/5 sessions for that reason). Placebo is same-symbol / same-minute / same-geometry random-day; a sign-flip and a
block-shift null were not run (the sign-flip is the mirror cells above, which all lose). Stock friction (2–5 bps RT)
is not subtracted; it would not change any sign except possibly `prior_low` long. v2 bars are `adjustment=all`
(production daily bars are split-adjusted; dividend adjustment shifts daily SMA levels by fractions of a percent).
No earnings filter in either production rule or here. `exchange_calendars` XNYS sessions; WBD ends 10-05.
